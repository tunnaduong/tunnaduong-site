Building small, focused tools has become a core part of my philosophy. Rather than bloated frameworks and kitchen-sink libraries, I prefer to craft tools that do one thing well.

This approach has several benefits:
- Easier to maintain
- Faster to build
- More satisfying to use
- Teaches you how things really work

Some of my favorite tools are the simplest ones.
