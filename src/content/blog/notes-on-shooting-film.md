I've been experimenting with film photography in Ha Nam, and it's been a rewarding journey. There's something magical about the unpredictability and tactile nature of film.

The light here is beautiful-especially during golden hour along the rivers. I've been learning about exposure, metering, and the importance of slowing down to really see what you're photographing.

Still learning, but loving every shot.
